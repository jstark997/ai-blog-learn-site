Gradient descent needs $\nabla_{\theta} L$. Backpropagation is how a network
computes it without differentiating the whole composed function by hand: apply
the chain rule layer by layer, reusing what the layer above already computed.

$$
\frac{\partial L}{\partial W^{(\ell)}}
= \delta^{(\ell)} \left(a^{(\ell-1)}\right)^{\top}
\qquad
\delta^{(\ell)} = \left(\left(W^{(\ell+1)}\right)^{\top} \delta^{(\ell+1)}\right) \odot \sigma'\!\left(z^{(\ell)}\right)
$$
